## Platform

web

## Stack

User requested TypeScript/Node 22, Next.js App Router, pnpm workspaces, PostgreSQL and Drizzle, with a persistent worker. Local loopback first; optional private remote OAuth.

## Users

A private researcher investigating tokens verifiably included in Binance Alpha, comparing timestamped flow evidence with realizable paper outcomes.

## Product Purpose

Test the user's early-flow hypothesis with reproducible observations and conservative simulations. A score is never a profit probability.

## Operating Context

Dense desktop research with mobile access. Scan the complete official registry, investigate a chain/contract identity, inspect transactions, watch evidence alerts, and record paper experiments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Alpha-only candidates. Membership, tradability, Spot and Futures are separate facts. No execution, signing keys, paid calls by default, invented evidence, fake live charts, or unsupported wallet identities. Missing chain coverage remains unavailable.

## Evidence on Hand

Only an initial README existed. Official docs inspected on 2026-11-15. Live runtime verification is reported in docs/DATA_COVERAGE.md; no historical membership archive or trading-performance claims supplied.

## Product Principles

Evidence before inference. Preserve observation and availability times. Treat transfers as transfers. Show unknowns. Private by default.
