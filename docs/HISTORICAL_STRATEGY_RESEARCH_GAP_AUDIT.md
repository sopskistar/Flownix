## Evidence-based turnover methodology (proposal, not a deployed classifier)

The [Bitquery investigation](https://bitquery.io/investigations/binance-alpha-points-farming) supplies a bounded quq case study, not a general wallet-intent label. Its reported repeated round trips and threshold-adjacent volume motivate tests. Raw data redistribution/API rights are not granted by a readable article; [Bitquery usage is resource/points based](https://docs.bitquery.io/docs/ide/points/). Do not start paid queries.

For every admitted chain/contract and UTC window:

1. Preserve canonical tx/log/block/time, actor-attribution confidence, raw token and quote amounts, route/pool evidence and first availability. Reconcile starting balance + verified buys − verified sells + non-trade inflows − non-trade outflows against ending balances. Keep fees, transfers, mints/burns and rebases separate.
2. Compute gross buys/sells and net acquisition in consistent token/quote units; never infer acquisition from gross volume. Record `abs(net)/gross`, rapid quantity-matched round-trip share, median duration, and retained acquisition after predeclared 1/7/30-day windows. Missing coverage produces null, not zero retention.
3. Compare repeated daily quote totals with contemporaneously published Alpha Points thresholds using a specified tolerance and quote valuation. Require persistence across days and combine with low net position/short duration. A near-threshold day by itself is weak evidence; apply the same tests to ordinary Alpha controls.
4. Exclude pools, routers, bridges, exchange operational wallets and same-entity moves from beneficial-holder attribution unless evidence resolves ownership. Consider inventory management, arbitrage and independent traders as alternatives. Shared funding does not prove common control.
5. Output descriptive evidence such as “high turnover / low retained position; incomplete owner attribution,” with confidence/coverage and contradictory observations. Never assert wash trading, farming intent or manipulation from this pattern alone. Version features, retain raw evidence and test whether filtering improves out-of-sample precision.

## What can be tested now

Current data support schema/trust-boundary tests, registry identity/membership reconciliation, observed-window integrity, deterministic swap interpretation, quote-based scenario arithmetic and descriptive candle behavior over the 20 covered pairs. The timestamp guards can be unit/integration tested. Such tests validate implementation, not prediction.

Retained large-wallet acquisition, verified exchange flows, fresh-wallet clusters, market-maker inventory, holder concentration, selling pressure, pool-liquidity changes, historic-wallet reappearance, listing catalysts, unlocks, market regimes and rally exhaustion remain **untestable as predictive strategies with current data**: one or more required feature histories, time-valid labels, complete coverage, controls and executable outcomes are absent. Simple price/volume hypotheses could be explored descriptively, but the sparse membership/quote history prevents a credible admitted historical Alpha performance claim. There is no meaningful out-of-sample pass and no forward paper result.

Reuse Historical Runner Lab, Performance Lab, wallet research, detection traces, investigation queue, immutable signal observations, source hashes, `chronologicalSplit`, Decimal scenario costs and the existing forward outcome worker. Do not rebuild “Strategy Lab” as another application. Add a strategy registry and train/validation/test/walk-forward evaluator only after data prerequisites are met.

Point-in-time enforcement currently protects specific signal and fill paths, **not every possible research query**. All future feature assembly must require event time, source publication where known, local first-seen/available time, identity-valid membership interval and label validity/knowledge interval. Unknown publication remains null. A historical backfill received today may be context but cannot become a signal known months ago. Snapshot latest-metadata joins are forbidden for training; version corrections and use as-of joins. Test late labels and relist gaps explicitly before claiming universal accuracy.
