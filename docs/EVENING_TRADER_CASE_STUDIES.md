## Required repeatable protocol

For each case: archive the post URL and original timestamp; resolve the token against a time-relevant official Alpha membership snapshot; verify chain and contract; fetch transaction receipts and canonical blocks; reconstruct address balances from complete transfer history or block-pinned historical calls; independently source any entity label; compare contemporaneously available market data; record earliest knowable evidence, later contradictory evidence, and a negative control. Mark missing data `unknown`, never zero. Do not cite a later social post as an earlier signal.
